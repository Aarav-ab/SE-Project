# Week 4: Integrating Citizen Portal Pages

## Objective

To incorporate the separately developed pages into the main Citizen Portal and establish a consistent navigation flow between them.

## Relevant Context

After developing the individual pages, the next challenge was to integrate them into the application. Pages that worked separately needed to be connected through the application's navigation and routing structure.

## Work Done

- Incorporated the developed pages into the main frontend application.
- Worked on organising the TSX components and integrating them with the existing project structure.
- Reviewed how users would navigate between the different Citizen Portal sections.
- Checked the relationships between the complaint submission interface and the complaint tracking interface.
- Tested the integrated application and identified errors in the navigation and page flow.
- Investigated issues arising from the integration of individual pages.

## Key Observation

Integrating the pages introduced problems that were not always visible during individual page development. Differences in component structure, navigation handling, and the way pages were connected affected the overall application flow.

## Problem-Solving Approach

- Examined the integrated page structure and navigation logic.
- Reviewed component connections and relevant routing or rendering behaviour.
- Identified areas requiring changes to ensure the pages could be accessed through the intended workflow.
- Continued refining the integration rather than treating each page as a separate interface.

## Outcome

The Citizen Portal pages were brought together within the main application. Integration issues were identified, and work continued on improving navigation and the overall flow.

## Plan for Next Week

Resolve remaining integration issues, refine the Citizen Portal pages, and review the overall user experience.
