# Week 3: Completing the Remaining Citizen Portal Pages

## Objective

To complete the remaining frontend pages and bring the Citizen Portal closer to the planned application design.

## Relevant Context

The previous week focused on starting the implementation of the designs. Several pages and interface elements still needed to be developed or refined before the portal could be integrated as a complete application.

## Work Done

- Continued developing the remaining Citizen Portal pages.
- Implemented the planned page layouts using React and TypeScript.
- Worked on the structure and presentation of citizen-facing features.
- Refined the styling of page elements to maintain consistency with the established design direction.
- Reviewed the completed pages for layout issues and missing interface elements.
- Organised the page implementations in preparation for integration into the main application.

## Key Observation

Completing individual pages was only one part of the development process. The pages also needed to follow consistent styling and component conventions so they could work together within a single application.

## Solution and Improvements

Reviewed the page implementations, addressed incomplete sections, and made styling adjustments where necessary. Attention was given to consistency between individual pages and the overall Citizen Portal design.

## Outcome

The remaining planned pages were completed to the extent achieved during this development phase, providing the separate frontend screens needed for the integration stage.

## Plan for Next Week

Integrate the pages into the main application and resolve navigation and workflow issues that arise when moving between them.
